01 / START WITH THE PROBLEM

A useful solution starts with context.

A brochure website and a web application solve different problems. One explains your services and helps a visitor contact you; the other may manage accounts, records and operational tasks. We decide which you need before adding complexity. For a service website, the priorities are understandable copy, purposeful page structure, responsive layouts, speed and clear contact paths. For a portal, we also define permissions, stored data, validation and failure states. My work on Kandrex informs the practical questions involved in business platforms, without suggesting that every client needs a communications system. Search metadata, semantic structure and sensible deployment support the finished experience; no website build can promise a particular ranking, traffic level or conversion rate.

03 / BEFORE WE START

Bring the problem, not a finished specification.

Share the current situation and your priorities. We’ll identify missing information together before agreeing to build.

  • Business goals, audience and requested pages
  • Your logo, genuine images and approved service information
  • Domain ownership, hosting access and examples of preferred style

You complete sensitive verification yourself. Never send passwords, PINs, tokens or OTPs in a message.

What you receive

The final checklist is agreed in the scope. If an external decision or dependency prevents completion, the status and the next available step are made clear.

06 / FEES, WITHOUT SURPRISES

Pricing & running costs

Quoted after page and feature review. Domains, hosting, paid tools and ongoing content work are separately agreed.

Before starting, we agree what is included, who pays third-party charges and what would require a revised quote. No fixed price or processing timeline is assumed.

Important limitations & privacy

Application backends need suitable hosting. SEO is a foundation, not a ranking guarantee. Client-provided content and permissions must be verified before publication.

Only necessary information is requested for the agreed task. Sharing, access and document retention are discussed before handling sensitive material. Read the privacy notes.

07 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you get in touch

Do I need a web application?+

If visitors only need to understand your offer and contact you, a well-designed website may be sufficient.

Can you include a contact form?+

Yes when a real delivery endpoint is in scope. Otherwise direct email and WhatsApp keep inquiries working.

Can I update the site later?+

A CMS, documented code workflow or maintenance arrangement can be selected according to your editing needs.
