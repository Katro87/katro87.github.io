01 / MAKE THE PROCESS CLEARER

The task, without the confusion.

A tax return is easier to approach when the information behind it is organized. I help list the income, expenses, withholding records and other information relevant to your situation, then identify gaps before portal entry. FBR Iris navigation can be explained while you control your own login and verification. Filing support is limited to client-authorized work where legally permitted; uncertain treatment, complex business matters or disputed figures should go to a qualified tax practitioner. Your approval matters before any declaration is submitted. This service does not promise savings, refunds or a particular taxpayer outcome.

02 / THE SCOPE

What I can help with

  • Income and expense information organization
  • Withholding records and missing-data checklist
  • Iris navigation and return-status assistance
  • Authorized submission support where permitted
03 / BEFORE WE START

Your preparation checklist

We’ll check the current requirements for your location, provider and task before submitting anything. Start with the information below; sensitive documents should use an agreed secure channel.

  • Tax year and the nature of your income
  • Relevant statements and supporting records
  • Your own account access and confirmation of figures

You complete sensitive verification yourself. Never send passwords, PINs, tokens or OTPs in a message.

04 / HOW IT WORKS

A clear sequence, with review built in.

  1. 01

    Identify the tax year and information needed.

  2. 02

    Organize records and flag incomplete figures.

  3. 03

    Refer complex treatment questions to a qualified practitioner.

  4. 04

    Guide portal entries and obtain your review.

  5. 05

    Retain official acknowledgments and check status.

05 / THE HANDOVER

What you receive

  • An organized filing-information pack
  • Guided portal preparation
  • Submission/status records when officially available

The final checklist is agreed in the scope. If an external decision or dependency prevents completion, the status and the next available step are made clear.

06 / FEES, WITHOUT SURPRISES

Pricing & official charges

Assistance is quoted after reviewing complexity. Taxes, penalties, official charges and qualified practitioner fees are separate.

Before starting, we agree what is included, who pays third-party charges and what would require a revised quote. No fixed price or processing timeline is assumed.

Important limitations & privacy

I am not a licensed tax practitioner. Never send login credentials or OTPs in chat. You are responsible for truthful declarations; no refund or approval is guaranteed.

Only necessary information is requested for the agreed task. Sharing, access and document retention are discussed before handling sensitive material. Read the privacy notes.
