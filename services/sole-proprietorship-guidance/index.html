01 / MAKE THE PROCESS CLEARER

The task, without the confusion.

An individual business and a separate corporate entity involve different responsibilities. I help you list the practical questions before choosing a route: how you will invoice, maintain records, use a business name and approach banking or tax registration. Relevant NTN/FBR steps and bank document requests are checked against your circumstances. Rather than claiming one structure is always better, we prepare a checklist of decisions that may need a qualified tax or legal adviser. The digital setup can then follow the structure you have chosen, with accounts and records kept under your control.

02 / THE SCOPE

What I can help with

  • Individual-business versus SMC questions
  • Relevant registration and banking checklists
  • Invoicing and recordkeeping organization
  • Basic business-name and digital setup planning
03 / BEFORE WE START

Your preparation checklist

We’ll check the current requirements for your location, provider and task before submitting anything. Start with the information below; sensitive documents should use an agreed secure channel.

  • Your business activity and current registration status
  • Intended banking and invoicing needs
  • Questions you want reviewed by a qualified adviser

You complete sensitive verification yourself. Never send passwords, PINs, tokens or OTPs in a message.

What you receive

06 / FEES, WITHOUT SURPRISES

Pricing & official charges

A guidance fee is quoted after review. Official charges, banking costs and professional advice are separate.

Before starting, we agree what is included, who pays third-party charges and what would require a revised quote. No fixed price or processing timeline is assumed.

Important limitations & privacy

No blanket legal or tax conclusion is offered. Structure decisions and registration requirements depend on your facts; complex issues need professional advice.

Only necessary information is requested for the agreed task. Sharing, access and document retention are discussed before handling sensitive material. Read the privacy notes.

07 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you get in touch

Is this the same as a company?+

No. An individual business differs from a separate corporate entity; specialist advice can clarify the consequences.

Will I need tax registration?+

We review the relevant official route and your current status rather than assume the same answer for everyone.

Can you organize invoices?+

Yes. Basic invoice fields and record organization can be included without claiming accounting services.
